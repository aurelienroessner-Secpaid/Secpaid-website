Why cannabis and CBD payments are high-risk

Medical cannabis and CBD sit on shifting legal ground, and payment providers price that uncertainty into every decision. The rules differ by country and by product. CBD faces novel-food classification in the EU and strict THC thresholds. Medical cannabis is legal with a prescription across much of Europe, and Germany extended legalisation to adult use in April 2024. What is compliant to sell and ship in one market may not be in the next.

On top of the regulation, many acquiring banks still associate anything cannabis-derived with recreational marijuana regardless of THC content, so they decline on category alone. Mainstream processors including Stripe, PayPal, and Square prohibit most CBD and cannabis outright. Merchants that do get approved often find the acquirer changes its policy months later and cuts them off with little notice, freezing funds and taking the storefront offline overnight.

Add elevated chargeback rates, mandatory age verification, and the scrutiny that comes with any health claim, and a single-provider setup becomes a real business risk rather than a convenience.

Frequently asked questions

Does SecPaid process payments for recreational cannabis?

The focus is licensed medical cannabis and legally compliant CBD and hemp. Adult-use depends on the jurisdiction and the acquirer, and your account manager confirms what is possible for your markets.

What documentation does a cannabis or CBD merchant need?

Business and product licensing, certificates of analysis for your SKUs, and evidence of compliance with local novel-food or medical rules.

Which countries can I sell into?

The acquirer network is EU-focused. You confirm your specific markets during onboarding.

Can a CBD brand that was dropped by another processor apply?

Yes. Prior termination is normal in this category and is not disqualifying.

Is a rolling reserve required for cannabis and CBD merchant accounts?

Often. The reserve is set by the acquirer based on risk, volume, and history, not by SecPaid.

Do you support age verification at checkout?

Yes. Age verification is integrated at checkout where the product or market requires it.
