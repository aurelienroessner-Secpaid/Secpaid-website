Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data, and your rights under the GDPR.

1. Introduction

Welcome to SecPaid. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data in an open and transparent manner. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit secpaid.com or use our services. By using the site, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Personal data. Information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, shipping address, email address, and telephone number, plus demographic details like age and interests.

Derivative data. Information our servers collect automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and the pages you have viewed.

Financial data. Payment-related information such as a valid credit card number, card brand, and expiration date, collected during transactions.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to maintain and improve our operations, analyse usage patterns, develop new offerings, provide customer support, send marketing communications where permitted, and find and prevent fraud and security issues.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information when legally required, in connection with a business transfer, or with your explicit consent for a specific purpose. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Data Security

We implement administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your data. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against interception or misuse.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your data, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to keep the site working correctly, understand how it is used, and personalise content. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

9. Third-Party Services

Our site may link to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for the content, accuracy, or privacy practices of any third party.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page and take effect immediately upon publication.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to contact@secpaid.com, by phone at +49 89 200014130, or via our contact page.
