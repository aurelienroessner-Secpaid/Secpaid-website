Real-time callbacks

  1. Customer paysThey open the link or checkout and complete payment with any method.
  2. SecPaid posts the eventYour callback URL receives the payment id, user id, and status within seconds.
  3. You acknowledgeReturn 200 OK. If not, SecPaid retries with exponential backoff and alerts you after the last attempt.

Common questions

Do you work with regulated industries like pharmacy, supplements, and cannabis?

Yes. SecPaid is purpose built around regulated categories, including online pharmacies, supplement and nutraceutical brands, and licensed cannabis and CBD retailers.

How fast can I start processing?

Most merchants are approved and live within 24 hours of applying. Underwriters review applications in your category every day, so your file does not sit in a generalist queue.

Who handles my chargebacks?

Your dedicated account manager does. They compile evidence, file the representment, and track your dispute ratio against card network thresholds on your behalf.

Does SecPaid hold my money?

No. SecPaid is software that orchestrates payments. Authorisation, settlement, and custody of funds are handled exclusively by the licensed payment providers and acquirers you connect.

Can I keep my current payment provider?

Yes. Connect your existing provider first, then add others when you want better rates, higher acceptance, or coverage in a new market. Routing rules decide which one handles each payment.

How do fees work?

You pay your providers their normal processing fees directly. SecPaid charges a transparent, volume-based fee on top, shown in the pricing table above, and never marks up processor fees.
