Why supplement and nutraceutical payments are high-risk

Card networks and acquiring banks classify nutraceuticals as high-risk, and most mainstream processors act on that classification aggressively. Stripe, PayPal, Square, and Shopify Payments routinely decline supplement applications or terminate live accounts without notice, often during peak season, and hold settled funds for months afterward.

The label comes from real patterns. Supplement brands run subscription and auto-ship billing, which produces "I did not authorise this" disputes and friendly fraud at rates well above general retail. Free-trial and continuity offers amplify that. Marketing claims, especially around weight loss, muscle gain, and cognitive performance, draw scrutiny from regulators such as the FTC in the United States and national authorities across the EU, and the acquirer inherits part of that liability. Affiliate-driven traffic adds another layer, because a claim an affiliate makes on a landing page still reflects back on your merchant account.

Certain ingredients raise the risk further or get banned outright by specific acquirers: hormone precursors such as DHEA, some nootropics, stimulant-based weight-loss compounds, SARMs, and anything positioned as a drug rather than a food supplement. A processor that does not understand these distinctions declines the whole application rather than working through the product list.

Frequently asked questions

Will SecPaid approve a supplement brand that Stripe or PayPal terminated?

Yes. Prior termination is common in this category. Underwriting looks at your current descriptor, chargeback history, product list, and marketing, not at whether an aggregator dropped you.

Do you support subscription and auto-ship billing?

Yes, with retry logic, dunning, and a customer portal for self-service cancellation, which is the fastest way to stop a dispute before it is filed.

Which supplement products cannot be processed?

Anything unlicensed or marketed as treating or curing a condition. Banned-ingredient lists vary by acquirer, and your account manager confirms your specific catalogue during onboarding.

Is there a rolling reserve on a nutraceutical merchant account?

Sometimes. Reserves are set by the acquirer based on volume, history, and product mix, not by SecPaid. Routing across more than one provider can reduce the reserve you carry overall.

Can I keep PayPal as a checkout option?

Yes, alongside cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and local methods such as SEPA and iDEAL.

How fast can a supplement brand start processing?

Most supplement merchants are approved and live within 24 hours.
