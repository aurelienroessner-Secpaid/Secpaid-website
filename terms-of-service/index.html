5. Assignment of Receivables

SecPaid acts as assignee of customer claims through an automated process that requires no additional signature. The assignment of receivables is a legal instrument used, in particular, in commercial transactions to collect receivables efficiently. SecPaid receives claims in its own name but is obliged, as a fiduciary agent, to transfer the collected amount to the customer's known bank account immediately.

5.1 Concept and Fundamental Meaning

The assignment of receivables is a term from civil law referring to the transfer of a claim for the purpose of collection by a new creditor. The new creditor receives the authority to assert the claim in its own name, on behalf of the original creditor.

5.2 Legal Basis

This fiduciary relationship obliges the assignee to collect the claim for the assignor's account and to comply with the assignor's instructions. With the SecPaid software, this process is fully automated, so the invoice amount, once credited within the SecPaid system, is paid out to our customer.

5.3 Legal Regulation and Demarcation

The authority to assign derives from Sections 398 et seq. of the German Civil Code (BGB). Unlike a full assignment, the collected claim is formally transferred to the assignee, but the assignee remains obliged to transfer the proceeds to the assignor.

5.4 Contracting Parties and Participants

Three parties participate in this structure: the assignor (the customer), the assignee (SecPaid Limited & Co. KG), and the debtor (the customer's own customer).
